NFL news roundup: Brandon Graham retires again; 49ers DT Alfred Collins out for season with torn patellar tendon

Around the NFL Staff
NFL.com

NFL.com keeps you up to date with all of the latest league news from around the NFL. Visit NFL.com's transaction hub for a daily breakdown.

  • READ: Week 1 injury report
Arizona Cardinals
2026

DEPTH CHART NEWS

  • RB Tyler Allgeier is listed as the team's No. 1 RB on its unofficial depth chart. Rookie Jeremiyah Love is No. 2.
Atlanta Falcons

ROSTER CUTS

  • DB Darren Hall
Chicago Bears

INJURIES

  • WR Rome Odunze won't practice Tuesday and is considered "day to day" with a different injury than the foot he dealt with last season, per HC Ben Johnson. The team is hopeful Odunze will be good to go Sunday against the Panthers.
  • CB Kyler Gordon (calf), who is on IR, is away from the team working with his own trainer, per NFL Network Insider Ian Rapoport. The plan is for Gordon to return to the team a few weeks into the season.

DEPTH CHART NEWS

  • OT Braxton Jones will start at left tackle against the Panthers on Sunday, per Johnson.

ROSTER CUTS

  • DB Dontae Manning
Cleveland Browns

SIGNINGS

  • WR Bub Means (practice squad)
  • DE Nadame Tucker (practice squad)

ROSTER CUTS

  • WR Bryce Oliver (practice squad)
  • P Wes Pahl (practice squad)
Houston Texans

SIGNINGS

  • QB Mark Gronowski (practice squad)
  • C Gavin Gerhardt (practice squad)

ROSTER CUTS

  • C Ka'ena De Cambra (practice squad)
  • DT Dominic Bailey (practice squad)
Indianapolis Colts

SIGNINGS

  • TE Kenny Fletcher Jr. (practice squad)
  • OT Luke Tenuta (from practice squad)

ROSTER CUTS

  • WR Coleman Owen
  • CB Jaylon Jones
Jacksonville Jaguars

ROSTER CUTS

  • QB Carter Bradley
Kansas City Chiefs

INJURIES

  • QB Patrick Mahomes (knee) has a "very good chance" to start against the Broncos on Monday night unless there's a setback in the next few days, coach Andy Reid said Tuesday.
  • OT Josh Simmons: There is a "good chance" Simmons won't play against the Broncos on Monday, per Reid. Reid added, "Josh is rehabbing and working his tail off. This isn't anything other than him having an issue with his back. It's a matter of time here ..."
Las Vegas Raiders

DEPTH CHART NEWS

  • QB Fernando Mendoza is listed as the team's No. 2 quarterback on its unofficial depth chart behind starter Kirk Cousins. Aidan O'Connell is No. 3.
Los Angeles Rams

OTHER NEWS

  • DT Aaron Donald will not travel to Australia and will be inactive for their Week 1 game against the 49ers in Australia (8:35 p.m. ET, Thursday kickoff), per head coach Sean McVay. ESPN's Adam Schefter and NFL Network Insider Ian Rapoport added the Rams want to give Donald more time before he makes his much-anticipated debut. Donald was listed as a limited participant for Tuesday's practice.

INJURIES

  • DE Myles Garrett, who has been dealing with a knee issue, will play against the 49ers, per McVay. Garrett did not practice Tuesday. 
  • LB Grant Stuard (hamstring) limited 
  • CB Jaylen Watson (toe) DNP
New England Patriots

SIGNINGS

  • CB Christian Gonzalez agreed to terms with New England on a four-year, $135 million extension, NFL Network and ESPN reported. New England announced the extension on Tuesday evening. 

INJURIES

  • RB TreVeyon Henderson (ankle) has been ruled out for Wednesday's season opener versus the Seahawks.
  • OL Ben Brown (knee) out
Minnesota Vikings

SIGNINGS

  • S Harrison Smith officially signed his contract to re-join the Vikings. Smith's deal was initially reported on Saturday. 

INJURIES

  • TE Ben Yurosekwas placed on injured reserve.
New York Jets

INJURIES

  • RB Breece Hall (groin) will practice Tuesday and is trending toward playing Week 1 against Tennessee, coach Aaron Glenn said.
  • DE Joseph Ossai (foot) remains week to week, per Glenn.
Philadelphia Eagles

RETIREMENTS

  • DE Brandon Graham announced Tuesday that he is "officially done" with the NFL. Graham, now 38 years old, came out of retirement last October to play nine regular-season games for the Eagles. The two-time Super Bowl champion DE logged three sacks, bringing his career total to 79.5 over 16 seasons.
San Francisco 49ers

INJURIES

  • FB Kyle Juszczyk (finger) was listed a limited participant for Tuesday's practice. 
  • TE George Kittle (Achilles) limited
  • DT Alfred Collins suffered a torn patella tendon in practice and will miss the 2026 season, head coach Kyle Shanahan announced. 
  • DE Nick Bosa (knee) limited 
  • DE Romello Height (ankle) limited 
  • LB Dre Greenlaw (Achilles) limited 
  • CB Renardo Green (hamstring) limited
Seattle Seahawks

INJURIES

  • WR Tory Horton (hamstring) is questionable to play in Wednesday's season opener against the Patriots.
  • S Nick Emmanwori (ankle) questionable
  • S Ty Okada(hamstring) out
Tampa Bay Buccaneers

ROSTER CUTS

  • DB Xavier Williams

SIGNINGS

  • QB Baker Mayfield signed a three-year extension, the team announced. The deal is worth $165 million, an average of $55 million per season on the new years of his deal, Mike Garafolo, Ian Rapoport and Adam Schefter reported.
