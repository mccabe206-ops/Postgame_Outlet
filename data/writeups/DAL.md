## The case for above average

Dallas grades above the line — a steady quarterback, an elite receiver duo, and a defense that took a real subtraction this offseason.

## Quarterback

Dak Prescott (+1.5) is an above-average starter grade — a steady, high-floor passer.

## What moved the number

- **Offense (+1.0):** raised from 0.5. The receiver room is a genuine strength — **CeeDee Lamb and George Pickens are an elite duo** — with MVS added for depth. The one thing holding it in check for the first month: **All-Pro left guard Tyler Smith is on IR** (torn thumb ligament, ~4–6 weeks), so the interior line is patched early.
- **Defense (−0.5):** cut from 0.0. Trading **Micah Parsons to Green Bay** removed a top-three edge rusher; Kenny Clark (who came back in the deal) and Rashan Gary don't replace that production. DaRon Bland, Malik Hooker, and Overshown keep the back end respectable, but league-average was generous after subtracting a player like Parsons.

## Bottom line

An elite receiver duo and a proven quarterback keep Dallas above the line; the Parsons trade and the temporary Tyler Smith absence are the reasons the overall number isn't higher.
