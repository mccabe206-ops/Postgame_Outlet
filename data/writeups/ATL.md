## Week 1: at Pittsburgh (L 13–20)

Atlanta opened at Pittsburgh a QB room in tatters and lost 13–20. Tua Tagovailoa, named
the Week 1 starter, was ruled out two days before kickoff with an **oblique injury**;
Michael Penix Jr. was inactive, still working back from a 2025 torn ACL. That left
**third-stringer Cooper Rush** to start — and he struggled: 12-of-22 for 143 yards, one
touchdown and **two interceptions**, four sacks. The rating now reflects Rush, not Tua.

## The case with the QB room down

Atlanta grades below the line — the quarterback spot is the weight, now down to its third
option, and the defense's pass rush is thinned through the first half.

## Quarterback

With **both Penix (ACL) and Tua (oblique) out**, **Cooper Rush (−3.5)** is the starter —
a full point below Tua's −2.5, a journeyman spot-starter whose Week 1 line (2 INTs) matched
the grade. Reporting has Rush likely starting Week 2 vs. Carolina as well. The grade restores
to Tua (−2.5) or Penix (−2.0) — Penix reportedly "really close" — once healthy.

## What moved the number

- **Quarterback (−2.5 → −3.5):** the listed starter changed. With Tua (oblique) out and
  Penix (ACL) not ready, Cooper Rush draws in a full point below Tua. Reverts up as the room
  gets healthy.
- **Offense (+1.0):** a genuine strength — Bijan Robinson is a top-tier back and Drake London a legit WR1, with Jahan Dotson and Kyle Pitts around them. It sits at the top of the normal band; the below-average QB caps how much further it can climb.
- **Defense (−0.5):** cut from 0.0. The edge rush is **gutted through Week 8** — **Jalon Walker is out for the season** (torn ACL, IR) and **James Pearce Jr. is suspended eight games** — leaving aging Ebukam and Ojulari to carry the pass rush. The secondary (Jessie Bates III, A.J. Terrell, Xavier Watts) is genuinely good and keeps the grade near respectable, but the first-half front is thin.

## Bottom line

Bijan and the skill group keep Atlanta competitive on offense; the negative rating is the quarterback spot, now compounded by a short-handed pass rush until the edge room gets healthy and reinstated around midseason.
