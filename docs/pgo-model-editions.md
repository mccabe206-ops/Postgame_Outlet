The earlier name **PGO v2** belongs to a separate roster-age and draft-pedigree
research experiment. It remained HOLD. Reusing that label for the corrected
edition would confuse two different calculations.

A model version identifies the calculation. A snapshot records specific inputs
and outputs. A source timestamp identifies when data was obtained; it does not
mean every performance observation occurred on that date. The corrected
edition's performance history ends with the 2025 regular season, while its
current roster and expected-QB inputs were captured on September 8.

New England remains first in the corrected snapshot. Recent results, QB passing
and team passing contribute to its output, and those signals overlap. A newer
snapshot or a new version name does not establish that the exact rank is right.
See the [corrected experiment and limitations](../research/pgo_week1_corrected/README.md).
