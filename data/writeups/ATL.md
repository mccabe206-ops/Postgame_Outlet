## Week 4: at New Orleans (W 45–24, MNF)

Atlanta beat the Saints by three scores in their building. The **competitive score (before it was
decided early in the 4th quarter) was ATL 38, NO 17**, and that's the right read. The 45 includes
garbage time, but the efficiency was real: **7.5 yards per play, 205 rushing yards, zero
turnovers, 4-of-5 in the red zone**. **Bijan Robinson** ran for 145 yards and 2 TDs (including a
59-yarder on the opening drive). **Brian Robinson Jr.** scored **three rushing TDs**. **Michael
Penix Jr.** was surgical: **15 of 20, 223 yards, a 31-yard TD to Jahan Dotson, no turnovers, an
81.0 PFF grade (7th of 33), 79.6 QBR and a 127.7 rating**. RG **Chris Lindstrom (93.6)** led a line
that dominated. The defense held New Orleans to **50 rushing yards**, and **Cameron Thomas had 7
pressures**.

**Injuries:** **starting LT Jake Matthews (groin)** left late in the first quarter and
**didn't practice Wednesday** ahead of a short week before Sunday night at Baltimore. **Michael
Jerrell (2nd string)** would be the effective starter, but he's limited (ankle) himself.
**Za'Darius Smith (2nd string → effective rotation starter** on an edge room without Jalon Walker
and the suspended Pearce) is in the **concussion protocol**. **Yasir Abdullah** (3rd string →
effective rotation, hamstring) didn't practice. Starter Cam Thomas (shoulder) was limited. CB1
**A.J. Terrell** (starter, groin, IR) is eligible ~Week 7, and **Mike Hughes (2nd string →
effective starter)** holds the spot until then.

**What changed:**
- **QB Penix −1.5 → −1.0 (+0.5):** two straight top-10 PFF games since coming back from the ACL.
  He's trending toward a league-average starter.
- **Defense −0.8 → −0.6 (+0.2):** shut down the run and got real pressure. Kept small with Smith
  in the protocol.
- **Offense held +1.0:** already the top of the band. **Drops −0.2 if Matthews misses Week 5.**
- **New total −1.3 → −0.6.**

## Week 3: at Green Bay (W 35–14, TNF)

Atlanta's first win, and a thorough one. **Michael Penix Jr.'s 2026 debut** — off the third ACL of
his career — was exactly what the room had been missing: 18-of-25 for 256, a touchdown and an
interception, a **73.0 PFF grade (8th of 30 QBs)** and an 84.9 QBR, and he said afterward he "came
out of there healthy." The engine was the ground game: **Bijan Robinson ran 29 times for 194 yards
and two scores**, **Drake London caught nine for 194**, and guards Chris Lindstrom (86) and Matthew
Bergeron (75) moved Green Bay off the ball for **242 rushing yards** and a 37-minute time of
possession. The defense's front — Cameron Thomas (89, sack), Maason Smith (87), Brandon Dorlus (86)
— held the Packers to 17 rushing yards, and Xavier Watts (85) added an interception. The box score
agrees this was real (ATL ~34 expected, scored 35); the one caveat is that Green Bay was badly
short-handed.

**Injuries:** none new for Atlanta. Still out: **CB1 A.J. Terrell** (IR, groin — earliest return
Week 7) and **DE Samson Ebukam** (hamstring, week-to-week), plus edge Jalon Walker (season, ACL)
and James Pearce Jr. (suspended, eligible ~Week 9).

**What changed:** **Penix −2.0 → −1.5** — a genuinely good first game back; a measured half-point
because it's one game and the run game carried it. **Defense −1.0 → −0.8** — the front dominated,
kept small because Green Bay's offense was depleted. **Offense held at +1.0** — dominant, but already
at the top of the normal band. **Total −2.0 → −1.3.**

## Week 2: vs Carolina (L 3–34)

A 34–3 home wipeout — but read it through the quarterback room. Third-stringer **Cooper Rush
was awful** (26.7 PFF, two more picks including a pick-six), and Atlanta managed just three
points. The weapons weren't the problem: **Drake London graded 83.4** and Bijan was fine — they
simply had no quarterback. **Michael Penix Jr. returns to start Week 3** (his 2026 debut off the
ACL), and the QB slot already reflects him (−2.0).

The lasting damage was on defense and the injury sheet: **CB1 A.J. Terrell — the secondary's
anchor — was lost to a concussion/groin and placed on IR**, and edge Samson Ebukam (hamstring)
went down on an already-thin front.

**What moved:** Cooper Rush −3.5 → −4.5 (dreadful, and against a Carolina defense that had just
allowed 59). **Defense −0.5 → −1.0** for the Terrell loss — held to a half-point for now, and
we're watching. **Offense held at +1.0** (the three points was the QB, not the unit; Penix is
back). Penix held at −2.0 — now a full 2.5 points clear of Rush.

## Week 1: at Pittsburgh (L 13–20)

Atlanta opened at Pittsburgh a QB room in tatters and lost 13–20. Tua Tagovailoa, named
the Week 1 starter, was ruled out two days before kickoff with an **oblique injury**;
Michael Penix Jr. was inactive, still working back from a 2025 torn ACL. That left
**third-stringer Cooper Rush** to start — and he struggled: 12-of-22 for 143 yards, one
touchdown and **two interceptions**, four sacks. The rating now reflects Rush, not Tua.

## The case with the franchise QB back

Atlanta still grades below the line — the quarterback spot is the weight even with Penix back,
and the defense's pass rush is thinned through the first half.

## Quarterback

**Michael Penix Jr. (−1.0)** is the starter, raised another half-point after a
127.7-rating, 81.0-PFF night at New Orleans (15 of 20, 223 yards, 1 TD). That follows his strong
2026 debut at Green Bay, coming back from the torn ACL that kept him out the first two weeks.
Behind him: Tua Tagovailoa (−1.5) at QB2 and **Cooper Rush (−4.5)** at QB3.

## What moved the number

- **Quarterback (now −1.5, Penix):** Penix reclaimed the QB1 slot at −2.0 in Week 3 and earned
  +0.5 with a 73.0-grade debut. Cooper Rush (−4.5) is back at QB3.
- **Offense (+1.0):** a genuine strength — Bijan Robinson is a top-tier back and Drake London a legit WR1, with Jahan Dotson and Kyle Pitts around them. It **held** through the 3-point Carolina game because that was the third-string quarterback, not the unit — London still graded 83.4. Sits at the top of the normal band; the below-average QB caps how much further it can climb.
- **Defense (now −0.8):** already cut to −0.5 for an edge rush **gutted through Week 8** (**Jalon Walker** season-ending ACL/IR, **James Pearce Jr.** suspended eight games, leaving aging Ebukam and Ojulari). Cut a further half-point in Week 2: **CB1 A.J. Terrell — the anchor of the one genuinely good group, the secondary — was placed on IR** (concussion/groin), with Ebukam (hamstring) also down. Nudged back up to −0.8 after the front dominated Green Bay in Week 3 (kept small for a depleted opponent).

## Bottom line

Atlanta's offense is legitimately good, and Penix is the reason it's no longer wasted.
Bijan and Brian Robinson, London and Pitts give him plenty to work with. The rating is still held
back by a short-handed pass rush and secondary until Terrell and the edge rushers return around
midseason, plus the risk that Matthews misses time at LT.
