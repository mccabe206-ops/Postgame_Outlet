# Defensive production and current depth evidence

Declared September 9, 2026, before construction or fitting. The descriptive
artifact covers all 32 NFL teams at its captured source clock. One player row
is a current team/GSIS identity. No issued forecast, rating, grade, or fitted
coefficient changes through this artifact.

Current roster and depth source bytes must be freshly captured with HTTPS URL,
UTC completion time, byte count, and SHA-256. The output states both capture
time and the newest depth observation no later than capture. An absent team,
unresolved identity, stale/missing position row, or missing player history stays
explicitly unknown. Depth labels retain the provider's wording; LB is not
automatically EDGE. Active roster status does not establish game availability.

The prior window is the complete 2025 season, including regular season and
postseason games in the pinned player, snap, and weekly-roster files. It is
strictly earlier than the current 2026 prediction season. Player history follows
stable GSIS identity across teams. Snap PFR identities resolve only within the
same historical team/week roster using the existing conflict-rejecting resolver;
unique normalized roster-name fallback is identified and counted. Ambiguous or
unresolved records cannot become zero production. Postseason resolution is
checked by this construction; the older identity qualification covered REG.

For each current defender, report observed defensive snaps and raw sacks, QB
hits, tackles for loss, passes defended, and interceptions. Statistics and snap
exposure must refer to exactly the same player/team/week records for a per-snap
rate. A missing statistic row/field makes the corresponding full-window rate
unavailable; it is never an observed zero. These are production/activity
measures, not pressure rates, talent grades, or value above a named replacement.

Current depth summaries count unique active defenders listed at rank one,
active defenders listed only below rank one, unlisted active defenders, and
listed backups with versus without observed prior defensive snaps. Players
listed at multiple slots count once. A player with no observed prior defensive
snaps has unknown demonstrated NFL production; this is not a low-quality grade.
Reserve and practice-squad players remain visible separately from active counts.

Outputs: a source-bound all-team JSON artifact, source coverage/identity receipt,
and tests for duplicate/conflicting identity, missing history, rookie treatment,
transferred history, postseason inclusion, snapshot timing, and exact exposure
matching. The artifact has status DESCRIPTIVE / NOT IN MODEL and null forecast
adjustment. No probabilities, team strength ranks, or calibrated injury values
are produced.

Any predictive candidate requires a separately locked feature formula and
evaluation charter before fitting. Current official depth ranks are not inserted
into historical games. A consistent historical/current ACT roster construction
using strictly prior production and observed role experience may be evaluated
as a diagnostic candidate under that separate charter. Historical publication
vintage remains REVIEW REQUIRED. Reused folds do not establish live performance.
