## Week 1: at N.Y. Giants (L 20–28)

An ugly Sunday-night opener — a 20–28 loss in which Jaxson Dart's Giants shredded the Dallas
defense (28 points, three touchdown passes). Dak Prescott threw a costly interception just
before halftime and finished a quiet 22-of-34 for 175, pressured nine times behind a line
already missing All-Pro left guard Tyler Smith. The defense was the real problem, and it got
worse: Dallas **lost two starters — safety Malik Hooker (forearm fracture, surgery) and
linebacker DeMarvion Overshown (hamstring)** — both expected to miss roughly one to two weeks.

## The case for above average

Dallas still grades above the line — a steady quarterback and an elite receiver duo — but the
defense has slipped below it.

## Quarterback

Dak Prescott (+1.5) is an above-average starter grade — a steady, high-floor passer.

## What moved the number

- **Offense (+1.0):** raised from 0.5. The receiver room is a genuine strength — **CeeDee Lamb and George Pickens are an elite duo** — with MVS added for depth. The one thing holding it in check for the first month: **All-Pro left guard Tyler Smith is on IR** (torn thumb ligament, ~4–6 weeks), so the interior line is patched early.
- **Defense (−0.5 → −1.0):** cut further after Week 1. Trading **Micah Parsons to Green Bay**
  already removed a top-three edge rusher that Kenny Clark and Rashan Gary don't replace — and
  then the unit got shredded by Jaxson Dart for 28 and three scores, and lost two starters:
  **Malik Hooker (forearm fracture) and DeMarvion Overshown (hamstring)**, both out ~1–2 weeks
  (PJ Locke and rookie Jaishawn Barham step in). Below average and thinner than the number was.

## Bottom line

An elite receiver duo and a proven quarterback keep Dallas above the line; the Parsons trade,
a leaky Week 1 defense now down two starters, and the temporary Tyler Smith absence are why the
overall number isn't higher.
