## Week 3: vs Atlanta (L 14–35, TNF)

A home-opening beatdown that exposed how much of this roster isn't on the field. Atlanta **out-rushed
Green Bay 242–17**, held the ball for 37 minutes and led 24–7 after three quarters. The box score
says it was closer to a 12-point game than 21 — a blocked field goal, a failed fourth-and-1 at the
Atlanta 7 to open the second half, and 2-of-4 in the red zone — but it was a clear loss either way.
**Jordan Love** threw 53 times (28-of-53, 312 yards, 2 TD, 1 INT) chasing the game, with a **46.7 PFF
grade (25th of 30)**, a 43.2 QBR and three turnover-worthy plays; he has now graded below average in
all three games. Matthew Golden (5/100, TD) and Christian Watson (7/96, TD) were the bright spots.

**Availability — the real story:**
- **Josh Jacobs has not played in 2026.** He has been on the **Commissioner's Exempt List since
  Aug. 30** (a misdemeanor battery case from a May incident, not an injury), and Ian Rapoport said
  Sept. 27 he does "not expect an imminent return"; a suspension is possible. *(This corrects our
  earlier write-up, which said he had won an appeal and would play Week 1.)*
- **RT Zach Bako-Bewele — season-ending knee injury** (Week 2). **LG Aaron Banks — out** (toe/knee, Week
  2). Rookie Jager Burton started at LG and graded 23; RT fill-in Anthony Belton graded 39.
- **WR Jayden Reed — neck injury (Week 2), no timetable**; Matt LaFleur said it's "way too early to say
  whether he can play again this season."
- **In-game:** RG **Jacob Monk — quadriceps**, left late in the fourth; LB **Edgerrin Cooper** was
  evaluated for a **concussion** in the second half. Green Bay signed OT Mekhi Becton on Monday.
- **Micah Parsons** remains on PUP (torn ACL), earliest return around Week 5.

**What changed:** **Love +1.0 → +0.5** — three straight below-average games, though much of it is
the protection. **Offense +0.5 → 0.0** — the +0.5 was built on Jacobs and a healthy line; neither is
there (no Jacobs, two starting linemen gone, Reed possibly done for the year). Golden, Watson and
Kraft keep it at average rather than below. **Defense −0.5 → −0.8**
— a terrible showing, run over for 242 yards; Cooper's concussion is a watch, Parsons is still the upside. **Total
1.0 → −0.3.**

## Week 1: at Minnesota (L 22–39)

New season, same script: Green Bay led 22–10 late in the third and then collapsed, outscored
29–3 the rest of the way in a 39–22 loss. Jordan Love piled up volume (21-of-42, 387 yards,
two touchdowns, including an 81-yard strike to Christian Watson, who finished with 147 and two
scores) but **two costly fourth-quarter turnovers** — a strip-sack and a tipped interception,
both forced by Brian Flores' blitz — flipped the game into short-field Minnesota touchdowns. A
failed fourth-and-1 sneak was the turning point. The Packers outplayed the Vikings for two-
thirds of it; this was self-inflicted, not out-talented, so the rating holds — the collapse and
Love's giveaways are the flag to watch.

## The case: talent on the sideline

Green Bay now grades below the line — not because the core talent vanished, but because so much of it is unavailable: Jacobs (exempt list), two starting linemen, Reed, and Parsons.

## Quarterback

Jordan Love (+0.5) is an average-plus, still-developing starter — cut a half-point after three
below-average games behind a failing line. The arm talent and flashes of top-tier play are there; the number stops short of elite because the consistency and durability haven't fully arrived. This is a quarterback the model likes and expects to keep climbing, not one it's ready to price with the game's best yet.

## What moved the number

- **Offense (0.0, was +0.5):** the preseason +0.5 assumed Josh Jacobs and a healthy line. Instead **Jacobs is on the Commissioner's Exempt List and hasn't played** (no imminent return), **RT Bako-Bewele is out for the year** and **LG Banks is out**, and **Jayden Reed (neck) has no timetable**. The backs have run for 37, 63 and 17 yards. Golden and Watson are real, and **Tucker Kraft** is back from his ACL, but this is an average unit at best until the line and backfield are restored.
- **Defense (−0.8, was −0.5):** cut 0.3 after Atlanta ran for 242 in Week 3. The grade is deliberately conservative and **has real upside on a delay**. Green Bay added **Micah Parsons — but he's on PUP recovering from a torn ACL and misses roughly the first four games** (a genuine game-changer once he's back, targeted around Week 5). Until then, **Hargrave and Franklin anchor the interior and Xavier McKinney is an excellent safety**, but with Gary gone the edge is thin early. The number reflects the September version of this defense, not the one Parsons joins.

## Bottom line

Green Bay is now below the line: a quarterback under siege, an offense missing its lead back and two starting linemen, and a defense waiting on Parsons. The number climbs back in steps — Parsons (~Week 5), Banks, and above all Jacobs, if and when the league clears him.
