## Week 1: vs Dallas (W 28–20)

The Giants opened with a statement on Sunday night, beating Dallas 28–20. **Jaxson Dart looked
like a Pro Bowler** (23-of-29, 230 yards, three touchdowns), Malik Nabers looked healthy and
back to WR1 form, Cam Skattebo ran hard, and the rebuilt offensive line held up — the offense
climbs to neutral off it. The pass rush did its part too, pressuring Dak Prescott nine times
(he went 0-for-8 under pressure) and holding a good Dallas offense to 20. A genuinely
encouraging night for a young quarterback and a reworked offense.

## The case for a rising rebuild

The Giants climb to the line — a second-year quarterback trending up, an offense that just
looked functional, and a genuinely dangerous pass rush.

## Quarterback

Jaxson Dart (-1.0 → -0.5) rises after a strong Week 1. Now a second-year starter (not a
rookie), he showed real command in the win (three touchdowns, an efficient line). Still below
average, but the arrow is up, and the number moves with it.

## What moved the number

- **Offense (-0.5 → 0.0):** up to neutral. **Malik Nabers** looked healthy and every bit a
  WR1 back from his 2025 ACL, **Cam Skattebo** gave the run game juice, and the **offensive
  line looks rebuilt** — enough to lift a group that had been priced for growing pains. The
  depth behind Nabers is still aging (OBJ, Mooney, JuJu), which caps it at neutral.
- **Defense (+0.5):** the best unit on the team by a distance. Reporting frames the **edge trio — Brian Burns, Abdul Carter, and Kayvon Thibodeaux — as one of the most dangerous front-end groups in the league**, with DJ Reader anchoring the interior. What caps the grade is the back end: the secondary is thin and dinged (Greg Newsome and Dru Phillips both banged up), so an elite rush is paired with a shaky coverage group.

## Bottom line

A fearsome front and a rising second-year quarterback are the reasons for optimism; an aging supporting cast behind Nabers and a thin secondary are why the number still sits right around the line. Week 1 was the kind of night that says it can climb from here.
