## Week 1: at Indianapolis (W 41–23)

Baltimore rolled — 41–23 at Indianapolis, 28 unanswered after the Colts' opening drive, a
506–251 yardage edge. Derrick Henry ran for 144 and three touchdowns, Lamar Jackson was in
full control, and the pass rush hit Daniel Jones nine times. It played like the elite rating,
and the offense earns a small bump for it — held modest because **Zay Flowers** (150 receiving
yards) left with a hamstring and will likely miss around a week. Also dinged: rookie WR
**Ja'Kobi Lane** (wrist, possibly a longer absence) and corners **T.J. Tampa Jr.** (knee) and
**Marlon Humphrey** (right leg).

## The case near the top

Baltimore is a top-five roster: an elite quarterback, a strong skill group, and a defense this model thinks is on its way back up.

## Quarterback

Lamar Jackson (+4.5) is a top-tier, MVP-caliber grade — the dual-threat engine that makes every skill piece play up.

## What moved the number

- **Offense (+0.5 → +0.8):** Derrick Henry and Zay Flowers are the elite headliners, and Week 1
  showed it — 506 total yards, Henry 144 and three scores. The move up is **only +0.3, not the
  full point the game suggested**, because Flowers (a 150-yard day) left with a hamstring and
  likely misses about a week; there's room to climb further once he's back and ramped. The one
  standing dent is the loss of **center Tyler Linderbaum to Vegas**, which softens the interior.
- **Defense (+1.0):** bumped up. The **talent is top-tier** — **Trey Hendrickson** and Campbell added to a core of Kyle Hamilton, Roquan Smith, Marlon Humphrey, Malaki Starks, and Nate Wiggins. Beyond the names, **it's this model's opinion that new head coach Jesse Minter is an upgrade for this defense**: it returns to the aggressive **Mike Macdonald style** the Ravens ran to great effect about three years ago, before Macdonald left to coach Seattle. The one caveat holding it at 1.0 rather than higher is **Justin Madubuike recovering from neck surgery** (his status is genuinely uncertain — possibly IR), which thins the interior rush to start.

## Bottom line

Elite quarterback, above-average offense, and a defense the ratings expect to climb under Minter's return to the Macdonald scheme. If Madubuike gets healthy, this is one of the most complete rosters in the league.
