## The case for a defense-carried team

Pittsburgh lands right at the waterline — a strong, veteran-laden defense balanced against an aging quarterback and a merely-fine supporting cast.

## Quarterback

Aaron Rodgers (−1.0) grades a shade below middling. At 42 he's clearly declined from his prime — the arm and the processing come and go — but he's still a capable veteran starter who can steer an offense and protect the ball. The number reflects a passer living on savvy rather than juice: enough to keep the unit functional, not enough to lift it.

- **Defense (+1.0):** the identity of the team. **T.J. Watt anchors a genuinely strong unit**, now with **Jalen Ramsey and Jaquan Brisker added** to the secondary (both Questionable but expected to play), plus Patrick Queen–style depth in Dean and Savage. ## Bottom line

The defense is real and does the heavy lifting; whether Pittsburgh climbs depends entirely on how much a 42-year-old Rodgers has left. As priced, it's a team that wins with defense and asks its quarterback not to lose games.
