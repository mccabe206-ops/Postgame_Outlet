## The case with Penix out

Atlanta grades below the line for now — the quarterback spot is the weight, with **Michael Penix out and Tua Tagovailoa starting**, and the defense's pass rush is thinned through the first half.

## Quarterback

With Penix out, **Tua Tagovailoa (−2.5)** draws in — rated a half-point below Penix's −2.0 as a startable veteran filling in. The grade reverts to Penix once he's healthy.

## What moved the number

- **Offense (+1.0):** a genuine strength — Bijan Robinson is a top-tier back and Drake London a legit WR1, with Jahan Dotson and Kyle Pitts around them. It sits at the top of the normal band; the below-average QB caps how much further it can climb.
- **Defense (−0.5):** cut from 0.0. The edge rush is **gutted through Week 8** — **Jalon Walker is out for the season** (torn ACL, IR) and **James Pearce Jr. is suspended eight games** — leaving aging Ebukam and Ojulari to carry the pass rush. The secondary (Jessie Bates III, A.J. Terrell, Xavier Watts) is genuinely good and keeps the grade near respectable, but the first-half front is thin.

## Bottom line

Bijan and the skill group keep Atlanta competitive on offense; the negative rating is the quarterback spot, now compounded by a short-handed pass rush until the edge room gets healthy and reinstated around midseason.
