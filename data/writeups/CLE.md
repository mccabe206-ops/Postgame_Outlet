## The case for a good defense, dragged down

Cleveland grades poorly, and it is almost entirely the quarterback. The defense is genuinely good and the line was rebuilt — but there's no passing game to reward any of it.

## Quarterback

Deshaun Watson (-2.5) sits at the worst-starter floor, and that may still be generous. This is his first game in roughly 23 months, coming back from two surgeries on a torn Achilles, layered on top of a pre-injury decline (poor efficiency, a losing record). He's the confirmed Week 1 starter over Shedeur Sanders, but the model is pricing a real possibility that the arrow keeps pointing down.

## What moved the number

- **Offense (-1.0):** the trench work is real — the **offensive line was genuinely rebuilt** (Fano, Zion Johnson, Elgton + Teven Jenkins, Tytus Howard) and **Jerry Jeudy is a legitimate WR1** — but there's no quarterback play to cash it in, the backfield is unproven, and the WR depth behind Jeudy is thin. A good line blocking for a broken passing game nets out below average.
- **Defense (+1.0):** the part of this roster worth taking seriously. They **traded Myles Garrett to the Rams and still reloaded — Jared Verse and Quinnen Williams** headline a dangerous front, with Denzel Ward locking down a corner. It's not without cost: **Jeremiah Owusu-Koramoah (JOK) is out for the entire 2026 season with a neck injury** — a real subtraction the +1.0 already absorbs, and the reason this isn't graded even higher.

## Bottom line

An above-average defense stuck behind the league's most negative quarterback situation. The line and Jeudy give the offense a pulse, but until there's competent QB play, the defensive grade is the only part of this roster to bank on.
