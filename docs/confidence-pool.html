What this tells you

For fixed picks and the rules above, assigning the most points to the highest win chances maximizes expected points. Equal win chances use the order you entered the games. Games do not have to be independent for this sum to work.

Expected points are a long-run average, not a promised score. Maximizing that average is different from maximizing your chance of finishing first against other entries.

PGO's saved margins are not yet established as reliable win percentages. A positive monotonic conversion from margin to probability preserves confidence ordering. It can improve probability estimates without changing which games get the most points.
