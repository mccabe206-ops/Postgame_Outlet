## Week 1: at Tennessee (W 23–10)

The Jets went into Nashville and controlled it wire-to-wire — a 23–10 win in which the
offensive line dominated, Breece Hall ran for 102 yards and a touchdown, and Geno Smith was
clean and efficient (19-of-24, 215). They held the ball nearly 22 minutes and never trailed.
The defense sacked Cam Ward three times and forced seven punts, holding Tennessee to 195
yards — though it lost safety **Minkah Fitzpatrick** (groin) in the first half (rookie WR
Omar Cooper Jr. also exited with an ankle). One caveat on the read: Tennessee looks like a
team the model had overrated, so the win is discounted accordingly.
