## The case for a bottom-tier grade

Miami grades near the floor across the board — a below-average unit at quarterback, on offense, and on defense.

## Quarterback

Malik Willis (-1.5) is priced as a fringe starter. There's mobility to work with, but this is a bottom-of-the-league passing situation, not a lever that lifts the roster.

## What moved the number

- **Offense (-1.5):** the receiver room is gutted — Hill, Waddle, and Wilson are out — leaving little proven separation around a limited passer.
- **Defense (-1.5):** lost Chubb and Fitzpatrick; Uche and Ojabo are the additions, but the talent bled out faster than it came in.

## Bottom line

A roster in transition with negative grades on all three inputs. It would take real overperformance from Willis to pull this number toward respectability.
